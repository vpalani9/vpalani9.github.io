StaffPay Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2025

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application StaffPay ("Application") for mobile devices that was created by VEL Software Technologies. The Application is intended to provide a digital solution for small business owners to manage their employee attendance, advances, and payroll settlements.

Information We Collect

The Application obtains the information you provide when you download and register the Application. Registration with us is mandatory in order to be able to use the basic features of the Application.

1. User-Provided Information

When you register with us and use the Application, you generally provide:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number for account creation and identification.
  • Information you enter into our system when using the Application, such as your employees' data. This includes employee names, phone numbers, salary details, attendance records, and transaction history (advances and settlements).

2. Automatically Collected Information

The Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile device's unique device ID, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, and information about the way you use the Application. This is done through third-party services as detailed below.

How We Use Your Information

The primary purpose of collecting your data is to provide you with a safe, efficient, and personalized experience. We use the information you provide to:

  • Operate and maintain the core functionality of the Application (e.g., calculating salaries, tracking attendance).
  • Secure your account and the data you store within it.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • Display relevant advertisements in the free version of the Application through Google AdMob.

We do not share the personal or employee data you enter into the app with any third party for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services and Data Storage

All the data you provide is stored and processed using Google's Firebase services. We rely on the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore Database): Used for secure user authentication and to store all user and employee data. All data is protected by Firebase Security Rules to ensure that you can only access the data you have created. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display ads in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect and use data to provide personalized advertising. Google Ad Policy

Data Security

We are committed to protecting your data. We utilize Google Firebase's robust security infrastructure to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes to our Privacy Policy by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
